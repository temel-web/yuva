## Product Purpose

Yuva is a native Temel demonstration store for furniture, lighting, rugs, decor, and bedroom
storage. Nothing is for sale. Prices are illustrative and the cart ends at a subtotal.
These facts come from `/Users/ilyas/git/yuva/README.md` and the existing authored pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing store provides categories, product pages, photography, 360-degree views, currency
selection, audience variations, and a cart saved in the visitor's browser. It uses native
custom elements and has no build step. The redesign changes the custom-element layouts, composition, and motion while preserving
those capabilities, all product facts, authored copy, and incomplete-content acceptance.
